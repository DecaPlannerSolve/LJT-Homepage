I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Prof. Junxian He. Prof. He also supervised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). I graduated from SJTU in June 2024 with a B.Eng. My research focuses on natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML).

## Research Interests

* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLMs)
* LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
